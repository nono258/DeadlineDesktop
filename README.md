**Deadline Desktop v1.7** includes:

- **Desktop dashboard:** Clock, major events, tasks due today, and deadline progress bars changing from green to red.
- **Task management:** Create, edit, and complete tasks; automatically cancel overdue tasks while keeping their records.
- **Customization:** Choose a background photo and adjust module names, positions, sizes, colors, and transparency. Individual tasks support separate styling.
- **Sorting and pagination:** Manual or automatic sorting, adjustable tasks per page, and configurable grid rows and columns for major events.
- **Data relocation:** Move events, photos, settings, and backups to another folder, verify them, then remove the old folder.
- **Everyday convenience:** Chinese/English options, startup at Windows sign-in, system tray operation, and window recall when reopening the app.

The installer is approximately **19.73 MB**, with no bundled background photos.
